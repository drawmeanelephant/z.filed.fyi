Qingyan (智谱清言) is Zhipu's consumer assistant for the Chinese market, and it is a deliberately separate product from the international chat.z.ai: its own domain, its own apps, its own release rhythm. For anyone tracking the company, it is the domestic half of the consumer story, and the thinner-sourced one.

## What it is

The assistant ships as a web app and mobile apps in China, built on the same GLM model line that powers the international surfaces. Zhipu lists it among its native products, and its public retrospectives treat it as the consumer face of the domestic platform. Press coverage records more than 25 million users in the 2024 era, and commercialization revenue growing by more than 100 percent year over year that year.

## Where the agent features premiered

Several of Zhipu's agent experiments reached users here first or in parallel with the international editions: deep-research and operator previews (the 沉思 line), and multi-agent collaboration features the company has folded into its platform story. The international equivalents surface through chat.z.ai, the Slide/Poster agent, and the API platform.

## What this guide could not verify

Honest gaps, listed rather than guessed:

- Current user numbers. The 25 million figure is from the 2024 era; newer official numbers were not found.
- The current feature set and version history. The app updates faster than the English-language record documents.
- English-language documentation. Most material on this product is Chinese-only, and several citations in this guide's company pages are domain-level for that reason.

This page will grow as the record does. The method is to carry what is sourced and to name what is not.
