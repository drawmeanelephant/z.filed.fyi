AutoClaw is Z.ai's desktop agent for work, and a practical answer to a simple problem: OpenClaw, the open-source personal-agent framework, is powerful but not trivial to set up. AutoClaw installs it in one step, adds a curated skill library and Z.ai's own browser automation, and routes the whole thing through GLM models.

## What it is

Z.ai describes AutoClaw as an "AI agent for work" and, in its documentation, as a digital employee built on the OpenClaw framework: install it locally in about a minute, with no development environment and no API key required, and then hand it goals instead of prompts. It runs on the desktop rather than in a browser tab, and its work happens through chat and IM surfaces rather than a single console.

The product launched on March 10, 2026, presented by Chinese tech press as the country's first true one-click local OpenClaw distribution; it has shipped continuously since (version 1.17.8, dated August 27, 2026, at this snapshot).

## The OpenClaw connection

OpenClaw is an independent open-source project, not a Z.ai product: a self-hosted personal agent framework created in November 2025 (it spent its early months as Clawdbot before trademark pressure pushed it through renames), MIT licensed, and now stewarded by a nonprofit foundation with broad corporate participation. AutoClaw is Z.ai's client for that framework; its changelog tracks OpenClaw kernel updates. Z.ai contributes the surrounding conveniences: preset skills, IM integration, and device-side automation.

## What it does

Z.ai organizes AutoClaw's capabilities into six areas:

- **Office automation.** Word, Excel, PowerPoint, reports, meeting notes, and structured documents, backed by 50+ built-in skills.
- **Content operations.** Multi-platform drafting and repurposing (Telegram and Instagram posts, Substack writing, X threads, TikTok scripts) from topic to schedule.
- **Investment research.** Market data, filings, strategy backtests, and structured analysis reports.
- **Web product building.** Describe a page, dashboard, or internal tool; AutoClaw writes runnable frontend code with a browser preview.
- **Browser automation.** Form filling, screenshots, web data collection, console checks, and scheduled browser tasks, powered by Z.ai's Browser-Use stack.
- **IM integration.** Assign work by mentioning the assistant in chat; results, files, and progress flow back into the thread.

Beyond those six areas, the app layers on multi-agent "cluster" workflows, a design agent that produces editable visuals, connectors for deployment platforms, scheduled tasks, and an in-app inbox. The model menu centers on the GLM line (GLM-5.3-Flash, GLM-5.3, GLM-5.2, and the Turbo variants), with hot-swapping to other providers' models also supported.

## Models, membership, and the Coding Plan

AutoClaw is closely tied to the GLM Coding Plan:

- New users receive a 200-million-token bonus on registration (stated value: 24 USD).
- Coding Plan subscribers who connect AutoClaw get a limited-time 150% quota boost, and a monthly login claims bonus credits (Lite 5,000, Pro 10,000, Max 26,000).
- Both individual and team plans are supported.

From September 3 to October 7, 2026, Z.ai ran an overnight campaign in which paid users could run GLM-5.3-Flash without consuming quota (23:00 to 09:00) through ZCode and AutoClaw specifically.

## Platforms and editions

- **Desktop.** macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and Windows 10 or later.
- **Chinese edition.** Adds KylinOS builds (x86 and ARM) alongside the standard platforms, plus a team-purchase channel.
- **Mobile.** iOS and Android companions that can view and trigger tasks; a web workspace also exists.
