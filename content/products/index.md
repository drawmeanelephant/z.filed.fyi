There is a consumer assistant, a coding subscription, a developer platform, and a set of agent tools, including AutoClaw, the company's one-click client for the OpenClaw framework. This page is the map: every first-party product, its status as of September 29, 2026, and the page in this guide that covers it.

## Covered in this guide

- **[Chat on Z.ai](../chat/index.md).** The consumer assistant at chat.z.ai, running on GLM-5.3-Flash, with agent-mode tasks, slide and poster generation, and web search built in. Status: live; the free experience is the default entry point.
- **The [GLM Coding Plan](../code/index.md).** A subscription (Lite, Pro, Max, and a Team Plan) that points GLM-5.3 and GLM-5.3-Flash at more than twenty coding tools, from Claude Code to Z.ai's own ZCode. Status: live; list pricing starts at 18 USD per month.
- **[AutoClaw](../autoclaw/index.md).** Z.ai's one-click desktop client for the OpenClaw framework: an agent for work with 50+ built-in skills, office and browser automation, and IM integration. Status: live since March 2026; desktop editions for macOS and Windows, plus a Chinese edition that adds KylinOS.
- **[ZCode](../zcode/index.md).** The first-party coding workbench and the official harness for GLM-5.3, shipped as a desktop app, a browser workspace, and a terminal agent. Status: live and openly developed (Apache-2.0) since September 2026.
- **The [developer platform](../api/index.md).** docs.z.ai: the OpenAI-compatible API with the full GLM menu, task-shaped agents (slide, translation, video effects), and per-token pricing. Status: live.
- **[AutoGLM](../autoglm/index.md).** The device-agent line: phone agents that operate real apps, the open framework, and the open models. Status: live; the framework and the 9B models are open source.
- **[Qingyan (智谱清言)](../qingyan/index.md).** The domestic consumer assistant at chatglm.cn; the public record is thinner than the international surfaces, and the page says so. Status: live.
- **[BigModel](../bigmodel/index.md).** The China-side developer and enterprise platform, the domestic mirror of docs.z.ai. Status: live.

## Beyond the product list

The multimodal and creative surfaces (GLM-Image, CogVideoX-3, GLM-OCR, GLM-ASR-2512, and the Slide/Poster agent) are priced and documented on the [API page](../api/index.md) and the model pages.

## How this list is kept

Every status line here is a dated snapshot (2026-09-29). The linked pages carry their own sources and record what they could and could not verify. When a door moves, this guide links the official page rather than mirroring it.
