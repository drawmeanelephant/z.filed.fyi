Zhipu AI (北京智谱华章科技股份有限公司, trading as Z.ai internationally) is a Beijing company that builds large language models, the products around them, and - since January 2026 - a public equity story.

## What it is

A research lab that became a platform company. The institutional facts: founded June 2019 as a spin-out of Tsinghua University's Knowledge Engineering Group, headquartered in Beijing, with an English-language product surface at z.ai and developer documentation at docs.z.ai. Its products span consumer chat ([chat.z.ai](../chat/index.md)), coding subscriptions ([the GLM Coding Plan](../code/index.md)), an API platform ([docs.z.ai](../api/index.md)), and enterprise offerings in the Chinese market. The [history section](../history/index.md) covers the research arc; this page covers the business.

## Capital history in brief

More than eight funding rounds and over 8.3 billion yuan raised before the IPO, with 87 shareholders on the register at listing - among them Meituan, Ant Group, Alibaba, Tencent, Xiaomi, and a roster of state-linked and venture funds. The trajectory: more than 2.5 billion yuan raised during 2023, a roughly 20-billion-yuan valuation by late 2024, and a listing in January 2026 that opened at a market capitalization north of 51 billion Hong Kong dollars.

Reported financials deserve equal billing with the market cap: an annual loss of roughly 4.7 billion yuan for 2025 was widely reported alongside the rally. This guide treats both as facts with sources, not as arguments.

## The consumer side

The domestic Qingyan app passed 25 million users in the 2024 era, and the developer platform reported millions of registered builders by mid-2026 (covered, with confidence labels, in [the history pages](../history/pivot.md)). Internationally, chat.z.ai and the coding plan are the visible front doors.
