2023 was the year Zhipu became famous and rich at the same time, and 2024 was the year it tried to turn that into a product company.

## The ChatGLM wave

Three and a half months after ChatGPT's launch, on the day GPT-4 was announced, Zhipu released ChatGLM-6B: a 6-billion-parameter bilingual chat model that could run on a single consumer GPU. The repository gathered tens of thousands of stars, and Chinese press retrospectives credit the model with more than ten million cumulative downloads - for a large slice of Chinese developers, it was the first LLM they ever ran. ChatGLM2-6B followed in June 2023 and ChatGLM3 in October 2023, while the hosted thousand-billion-scale model served the product side.

## The funding sprint

The valuation curve of 2023 explains the company's confidence through everything that followed. Zhipu announced more than 2.5 billion yuan raised during 2023 alone, with investors including Meituan, Ant Group, Alibaba, Tencent, Xiaomi, Kingsoft, and state-linked funds. By October 2023, reporting put its valuation roughly seven times higher than a year earlier. Through 2024 three more rounds landed, and by September 2024 the company was described as a roughly 20-billion-yuan "super unicorn." It also built consumer reach: the Qingyan app passed 25 million users that year, and commercialization revenue grew more than 100 percent year over year.

## The GLM-4 DevDay

On January 16, 2024, at its DevDay event, Zhipu announced GLM-4 - a new base model that press coverage framed as the domestic entry closest to GPT-4 class, with company claims of major gains over the ChatGLM line. The two-track strategy crystallized here: hosted GLM-4 tiers for products, smaller open weights for the community. June brought GLM-4-9B and GLM-4V-9B under open licenses; August's KDD announcements added GLM-4-Plus, GLM-4V-Plus, and CogView3-Plus on the hosted side.

## What it added up to

By the end of 2024 Zhipu had a research reputation, a consumer app with tens of millions of users, an enterprise platform, a full modality shelf, and a funding history longer than most listed companies. What it did not yet have was a flagship that global developers compared to the western frontier. That is the story of [2025](pivot.md).
