If 2023 made Zhipu known and 2024 made it rich, 2025 made it unavoidable. The thesis of the year: ship open weights at frontier-adjacent quality, price them like a commodity, and let Claude Code compatibility do the distribution.

## July: GLM-4.5 and a new name

On July 28, 2025, Zhipu released GLM-4.5 and GLM-4.5-Air as "ARC" foundation models - agentic, reasoning, coding - with doubled parameter efficiency and, critically, one-click compatibility with the Claude Code framework. The same week, the company rebranded its international platform as Z.ai, picking a name that travels better than "Zhipu Huazhang" and setting up an English-language product surface: z.ai, chat.z.ai, and later code.z.ai.

## August through December: the cadence

- August 11: GLM-4.5V, a 100B-scale open vision reasoning model covering video understanding, grounding, and GUI agents.
- August 8: the GLM Slide/Poster agent enters beta.
- September 30: GLM-4.6, positioned as the leading coding model in China and MIT licensed at 355B parameters.
- December 8: GLM-4.6V, vision with 128K context.
- December 22: GLM-4.7, an open-source state-of-the-art coding and reasoning release, shipped days before Christmas.

## The business behind it

The strategy was expensive and it worked. A reported three-billion-yuan round arrived in 2025 with commercialization revenue already growing at triple-digit percentages. The developer platform grew into the millions of registered users, and BigModel, the enterprise MaaS platform, reported the sort of "billion-era" token numbers that sound like marketing until the open-platform leaderboards back them up. Meanwhile the company was visibly preparing for public markets: reporting through late 2025 described a Hong Kong listing as imminent.

## Why it mattered

Open weights did three jobs at once for Zhipu. They were marketing (every fine-tune is an ad), recruiting (researchers want their work used), and a hedge (once weights are out, no license dispute takes them back - though the [2026 licensing turn](../models/glm-5-3.md) shows even that has limits). By January 2026, the strategy had a listing to show for it, and the model line had entered [its engineering era](engineering-era.md).
