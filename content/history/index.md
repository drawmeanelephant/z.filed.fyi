## Why the dates matter

Zhipu's history is unusually well-documented for a private AI lab: the release notes are public, the model cards carry dates, and since January 2026, filings and disclosures add another layer. This guide dates everything it can and labels everything it cannot; the [about page](../meta/about.md) explains the method.
