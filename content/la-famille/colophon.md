This guide is assembled from public sources, generated by open software, and set in licensed typefaces. The receipts:

## Software

- **Generator:** [la-famille](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/la-famille), a static site generator in Go. This site runs it unmodified; the theme is site-local.
- **Build:** no JavaScript build step, no framework, no package manager at runtime. The generator binary plus the repository's source files are the whole toolchain.

## Typefaces

- **General Sans** (Indian Type Foundry, via Fontshare), variable weights 200-700, under the Fontshare Free Font License. Ships in `assets/fonts/` with its license text.
- **JetBrains Mono**, variable, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Same directory, same rules.
- Chinese text renders in the reader's system CJK fonts (PingFang SC, Noto Sans CJK SC, Microsoft YaHei among them) rather than a downloaded webfont, a deliberate weight trade-off.

## Artwork

Three abstract images ship with the site (hero light, hero dark, and the node-link study on the previous page). They were generated as original artwork for this project; no stock photography or third-party imagery is used anywhere on the site.

## Sources and method

Every content page lists its sources. Official Z.ai and Zhipu documentation and model cards are treated as primary; press reporting is cited as such; community reports are labeled and used sparingly. Numbers are snapshots with check dates where they move (prices, star counts, market data).
