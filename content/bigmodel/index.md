BigModel (bigmodel.cn, with its open platform at open.bigmodel.cn and documentation at docs.bigmodel.cn) is Zhipu's developer and enterprise platform for the Chinese market. If docs.z.ai is the international door to the same models, BigModel is the domestic one: separate accounts, separate terms, and a catalog tuned to Chinese teams.

## What it offers

- **The model APIs, under domestic terms.** Chat-completion endpoints for the GLM line with the same family of capabilities the international platform prices: streaming, tool use, structured output, context caching, and the multimodal endpoints.
- **The GLM Coding Plan, China edition.** The same Lite, Pro, and Max structure with its own notice pages, which is where the September 3 to October 7 overnight campaign was also announced, plus team purchasing.
- **Agent tooling.** Documented agent APIs including managed agents (beta), the Slide/Poster family, and plan extras such as Zread and the vision, search, and reader MCP servers; a GLM for Excel add-in appears in the benefit lists.
- **Enterprise deployments.** Private and managed offerings for companies, described across the platform's materials.

## Numbers and milestones

Two reported figures stand out: token volumes that entered what the company called the "billion era" in the first half of 2025, and community-sourced estimates of registered developers in the millions by mid-2026. Both carry lower confidence than the international platform's published numbers; treat them as reported.

## Why it matters here

Two reasons. Practical: several facts in this guide (the coding plan's campaign terms, agent documentation) were cross-checked against BigModel's pages, which sometimes carry details alongside or before the international ones. Editorial: the domestic-international split is a defining feature of Zhipu's business, and BigModel is the half the English-language web rarely covers.

## Honest limits

Most of BigModel's documentation is Chinese-only; pricing and catalogs differ from the international platform; and independent coverage of its numbers is thin. Where this page reports a figure, it carries the source and the caveat.
